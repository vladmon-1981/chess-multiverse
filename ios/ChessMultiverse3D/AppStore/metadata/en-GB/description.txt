ChessGM is 3D chess for the whole family: real three-dimensional pieces, four fun universes and move hints that help beginners. Play a friend on one screen, the computer on three levels or online via Game Center. It works offline too.

FOUR UNIVERSES, FOUR SETS OF PIECES
• Classic: Staunton pieces in ivory and ebony on a maple and walnut board.
• Racing: cartoon cars with eyes on the windshield. Pieces drive, turn and crash.
• Vet Clinic: big-headed animal characters, staff against anomalies.
• Hockey: a rink with real markings, boards and stands. Pieces skate, and checkmate is a goal.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Computer opponent on three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. The engine runs on your device.
• Two players on one device, with optional board turning after each move.
• Online via Game Center: a friend or a random opponent, 30 seconds to 5 minutes per move, rating and leaderboards.
• Legal moves and the last move are highlighted; undo a move, flip the board, resign or start over at any time.

FULL CHESS RULES
Castling, en passant, promotion with a choice of piece, check, checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material. Move the pieces by tapping or by dragging.

A CAMERA THAT FOLLOWS YOU
Rotate the board 360 degrees, tilt it, zoom with a pinch. A camera button cycles through ready-made angles; switch on cinematic mode and captures play in slow motion.

SOUND AND HALL OF FAME
A neural-voice announcer, female or male, commentates the game in every universe. Wins earn points in the Hall of Fame on your device.

CHESSGM PRO
You can play 5 games a day for free. A one-time ChessGM Pro purchase removes that limit for good: no subscription, no ads.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
Playing the computer or a friend on one device needs no internet; only online games do. No accounts of our own, no ads and no tracking.

The interface is available in English and Russian and follows your device language.
